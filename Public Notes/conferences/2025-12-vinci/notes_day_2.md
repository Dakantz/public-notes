Authors: *Valeria Garro, Ilir Jusufi, Shahrooz Abghari, Jens Brage and Veselka Boeva*

- Parallel Aggregated Ordered Hypergraph (PAOH) - rather than animation ...

### Interactive Exploration of Approximate Solutions for On-Demand Ride-Sharing Transportation
Authors: *Shigeo Takahashi, Ryoya Yoshimoto, Yuki Tanioka and Kazuo Misue*
- Animation of pick-up over street graph
- Analyze combinations of multiple pick-ups/travel requests - optimization of vehicle assignments
- Cost: passenger time, travel time, ... - and feasibility wrt. pick-up/drop-off orders
- interactive placement/simulation of pick-ups/deliverables

### Comparative Views on Eye Movement Data Variables
Authors: *Michael Burch, Tamara Nyffeler, Serge Pellegatta and Sharon Reiser*
- Visualization of eye tracking: different approaches, e.g. heatmaps, scatterplotsm, bar charts, boxplots
- Again: a bachelor's thesis!


### SAHNet: A Spectral-Aware Hashing Network for Large-Scale Fine-Grained Food Image Retrieval
Authors: *Junsong Wang, Yihan Wang, Bolin Yang, Guorui Sheng and Lili Wang*
- Subtle (visual) food difference for retrieval - small inter-class variation, high intra-class variations
- Utilize multi-scale representation for effective retrieval; sub-models:
    - Spectral graph vs.
    - Spatial graph

### Developmental Aesthetics: Spatial Cognition and Children’s Evolving Appreciation of Abstract Patterns and Paintings
Authors: *Pengcheng Wang, Yuanqian Gao and Rongrong Chen*
